When the other side
won't sign, the court does.

A Yellow state channel moves money only on states both sides signed. If the node goes silent after the session, the user's settlement is stuck at the last state both signed. Countersign is a Nitrolite signature validator: Internet Court reads the terms both committed to, checks the cited source, and when the terms require the settlement, its countersignature stands in for the silent side on Yellow's own ChannelHub.

THE GAP

Yellow's own Internet Court skill notes it: a Nitrolite session "has no dispute mechanism, challenge, or timeout of its own". A refusal freezes the money.

THE HOOK

Nitrolite lets a channel approve extra signature validators. A channel that approves Countersign accepts the court's signature for the side that refuses.

THE LIMIT

The court signs only the exact state the terms require, only in that channel, only for the silent side. Everything else it refuses, often in code before any judge reads a word.

How it works

Five steps, one of them a court.

Everything except the judgement happens on Yellow's ChannelHub, byte-identical to Yellow's own Base Sepolia hub. The court only answers one question: which answer does the source show.

01
Commit

The first state, signed by both, carries the hash of the terms: a question, a source, a user share per answer.

createChannel
02
Silence

The session ends. One side proposes the settlement; the other never signs it.

no signature
03
File

Anybody files the anchor, the signed settlement and the terms on Countersign, which checks them against the hub.

Countersign.fileCase
04
Rule

GenLayer validators read the source and agree on the answer; the shares are compared in code. Final: SIGNED or REFUSED.

court.rule
05
Enforce

The case id is the countersignature. The hub accepts it for the silent side, and the channel closes or checkpoints.

closeChannel
Try it

Open a channel with a node that goes silent.

Your channel

Every step is a real transaction on GenLayer Chain or GenLayer Bradbury.

1
Get 10 test dollarsFrom the node's faucet, if your wallet holds fewer than 10.
2
Open a channel on a betYou deposit 10 tUSD on Yellow's hub; the first state commits to the bet. The node signs the opening.
Either way: if the answer is Yes, you get 10 back; if No, you get 2 and the node 8.
3
Claim your shareYou sign a CLOSE and ask the node to countersign it. It will not.
4
File the caseCountersign checks your anchor, your signed CLOSE and the terms against the hub.
5
Ask Internet CourtValidators read the source and agree in a minute or two. The verdict is final, and reaches the hub, after its half hour appeal window.
6
Close without the nodeSIGNED: the case id stands in for the node's signature and the hub pays you. REFUSED: the hub will not take it; claim again.
